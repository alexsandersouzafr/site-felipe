# Product

## Goal

Present the conductor's work, facilitate professional contact, and let the conductor maintain content without relying on development work.

## Experience Principles

- The visual language must feel elegant, editorial, and appropriate for a classical-music professional.
- Every public and administrative screen must be responsive, usable, and legible from small mobile screens through large desktop displays.
- Motion must enhance the experience without affecting keyboard navigation, performance, or users who prefer reduced motion.

## Public Pages

- Home: editorial summary, upcoming events, and entry points to primary content.
- Blog: posts built from paragraph, image, and YouTube video blocks.
- Bio: complete biography and highlights.
- Schedule: upcoming concerts and past engagements.
- Videos: embedded YouTube videos.
- Photos: image gallery.
- Contact: configurable contact details and form.

## Administrative Panel

The panel at `/admin` is a custom experience for one account. It manages content, publication, translations, and media.
