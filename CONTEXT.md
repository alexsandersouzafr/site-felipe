# Conductor Website — Domain Context

## Glossary

- **Conductor**: the sole administrator who maintains the website content.
- **Public content**: a published record visible on the website, as opposed to a draft.
- **Translation**: a field version in `pt`, `en`, or `fr`. When `en` or `fr` is unavailable, the public experience uses the Portuguese value.
- **Event**: a performance, concert, or engagement listed in the schedule.
- **Featured event**: an event the conductor curates (`is_featured`) to appear on the home page; not a visitor-facing concept.
- **Highlight**: a concise editorial item summarizing an achievement by the conductor.
- **Press kit**: the `/imprensa` page — high-resolution, credited photos plus the bio summary, for journalists.

## Product Rules

- Portuguese is the canonical and required language.
- Only the authenticated conductor can create, edit, publish, or delete content.
- Unpublished records and contact messages are not public.
